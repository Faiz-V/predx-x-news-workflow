# Internal product context

Use this only to improve internal story selection or a separate editorial note. Do not automatically insert it into either the Chinese mother draft or the public English copy.

PredX is a trading-intelligence and research layer, not a trading bot. Relevant product concepts include:

- News: discover and organize consequential information.
- Events and Market: connect an event with affected market questions.
- News-to-Market Match: identify which live questions a development may affect.
- Why It Moved: explain a change using attributable evidence.
- Resolution Verdict: assess resolution conditions and evidence.
- Smart Money Validation and Wallet Score: evaluate participant behavior and track record.
- Signal: prioritize research attention; never present it as financial advice or certainty.
- Search: retrieve related events, entities, markets, and historical context.

Public news posts should normally stand on their own. Product context may influence which story is selected or what next checkpoint is highlighted, but it should not turn routine news into a PredX advertisement.
