# Human publication checklist

Use this checklist after the workflow returns `READY` or `REVIEW`. A machine status never replaces editorial approval.

## Evidence

- [ ] Open every primary and supporting source directly.
- [ ] Confirm the central event, numbers, names, units, quotation wording, and procedural stage.
- [ ] Confirm the source time and the age of the material fact—not merely the repost time.
- [ ] Verify that X demand is current and independently echoed when the scheduled contract requires it.
- [ ] Preserve uncertainty and attribute single-source or disputed claims.

## Copy

- [ ] Confirm the Chinese version is the final mother draft.
- [ ] Compare Chinese and English block by block.
- [ ] Match every material number, named entity, qualifier, causal limit, and conclusion.
- [ ] Remove source metadata, internal notes, and accidental URLs from the post body.
- [ ] Confirm the account lens fits the story and the wording does not imply financial advice or certainty.

## Account and disclosure

- [ ] Confirm the destination account is an authorized PredX-controlled account.
- [ ] Confirm the account bio and relevant communication disclose affiliation.
- [ ] Do not frame another controlled account as an independent user or endorsement.
- [ ] Check that the post does not imitate a real individual or claim personal experience it cannot substantiate.

## Release

- [ ] Resolve every `REVIEW` caveat before publication.
- [ ] Do not publish a `HOLD` item.
- [ ] Run `lint_output.py` on the final structured package.
- [ ] Perform the final platform preview manually.
- [ ] Publish through an authorized human-controlled surface; do not automate engagement actions.
